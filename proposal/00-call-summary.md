# Call summary — PPPA-2026-YOUTH-SOCIAL-MEDIA-DESIGN

Source: Call document V1.0, 29.06.2026. This file is a working distillation, not a
substitute for the call document, the Model Grant Agreement, the Online Manual or the
Annotated Grant Agreement (AGA).

## 1. What the Commission wants funded

> Through co-creation under youth participation, design and test a Fediverse-based /
> Authenticated Transfer (AT) Protocol-based social media platform targeted specifically
> at young EU citizens and focused on user well-being, and adapt a roll-out strategy
> based on market research and stakeholder consultation.

### Specific objectives (all six must be addressed)

| # | Specific objective | Hard requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SO1 | Youth involvement in conceptualisation, with expert support (mental health, cognitive science, software development) and civil society | Young citizens from **≥ 8 Member States**, N/E/W/S balanced; incl. disabled and neurodivergent participants |
| SO2 | Market conditions analysis + uptake and sustainability strategy | Stakeholder mapping; stakeholders involved in building the strategy |
| SO3 | Build the software | **≥ 1 functioning prototype**, or upgrade **≥ 2 existing services**, on ActivityPub or AT Protocol |
| SO4 | Test and refine | Safety, usability, mental health; usability barriers; systematic feature comparison vs. other platforms; responsible AI |
| SO5 | Promote across EU to users and stakeholders | Implement **at minimum the first step** of the uptake strategy |
| SO6 | Document and share lessons learnt | **≥ 1 report** on opportunities/challenges + recommendations for future public funding; open-source results where possible |

### Scope emphases

- Decentralised data management; privacy, autonomy, wellbeing by design.
- The role of AI must be **prominently explored** — both as harm and as mitigation
  (discovery, moderation, verification, new user experiences).
- Look and feel / UI appeal to young audiences must be co-created, not only features.
- Co-creation must accompany **all** stages: ideation, development, testing, adoption.
- Free choice of co-creation format (workshops, living labs, focus groups, hackathons…),
  but the proposal must show how youth voice actually shapes the product.
- Protocol choice is free (ActivityPub, AT Protocol, or both) but must be justified as
  technically sound and most likely to meet the objectives.
- Collaboration with the two 2025 "Building a trustworthy social media sphere" awardees
  may be explored.
- Code open-sourced under the most appropriate licence wherever possible.

### Eligible activity types

Youth co-creation and consultation · software development · market research and economic
analysis · stakeholder mapping and engagement · awareness and dissemination · activities
seeking usage traffic and engagement (creator, media, education, youth-community pilots).

### Not eligible

- Financial support to third parties.
- Activities directed at EU institutions (lobbying, advocacy material, position mapping).

## 2. Money and time

- Call budget EUR 1 480 000; **1** project expected; max. requested grant EUR 1 480 000.
- EU co-financing **≤ 80 %** of total eligible costs → the consortium must bring ≥ 20 %.
- Duration normally 12–24 months; longer possible if duly justified; extensions by amendment.
- Indicative: evaluation from 7 October 2026, results Nov–Dec 2026, GA signature
  Jan–Feb, start Feb–Mar. (The call document prints these last dates as 2026; read as the
  cycle following the October 2026 deadline and confirm on the Topic page.)

## 3. Consortium composition (eligibility, not scoring)

- **≥ 4 applicants** (beneficiaries, not affiliated entities), **≥ 4 independent entities
  from ≥ 3 different eligible countries**.
- At least one entity each with demonstrated capacity in:
  1. youth engagement,
  2. software development,
  3. supporting the scaling of businesses or non-profits.
- Eligible countries: EU Member States (incl. OCTs). Natural persons (except sole traders),
  international organisations and EU bodies (except the JRC) are **not** eligible.
- Universities: supportive roles only, **≤ 1/4 of applicants**, **never coordinator**.
- Encouraged: disciplinary variety (mental health, digital rights, civic tech, AV/news
  media, content creation) and geographical balance.
- Affiliated entities and associated partners do not count toward the minimum.

## 4. Documents to submit

- Part A (online): administrative data + summarised budget.
- Part B (template from the Submission System): technical description, **≤ 70 pages**.
- Mandatory annexes: detailed budget table/calculator; standard CVs of the core project
  team; list of key projects of the last 4 years (template in Part B).
- Activity reports of last year: **not applicable**.
- Mandate to act for all applicants confirmed at submission; declarations of honour before
  grant signature.

## 5. Award criteria

| Criterion | Threshold | Max |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevance — clarity/consistency, fit with call objectives, EU strategic and legislative context, European/transnational dimension, multi-country interest and transferability | 21 | 40 |
| Quality — logical frame, consortium and team quality (incl. operational capacity), cooperation and problem-solving procedures, methodology (work organisation, management, subcontracting, timetable, risks, monitoring), cost effectiveness | 21 | 40 |
| Impact — ambition and long-term impact, dissemination strategy, sustainability after EU funding | 11 | 20 |
| **Overall** | **60** | **100** |

Ex aequo tie-break: Relevance → Impact → Quality → portfolio considerations.
