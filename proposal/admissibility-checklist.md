Tick before submission. Items marked **KO** are knock-out: failing one means rejection
without evaluation.

- [ ] **KO** Forms used are the ones inside the Submission System (not the Topic-page copies).

## B. Eligibility (call section 6)

- [ ] **KO** All beneficiaries and affiliated entities established in an EU Member State
      (incl. OCTs).
- [ ] **KO** ≥ 4 applicants (beneficiaries, not affiliated entities).
- [ ] **KO** ≥ 4 independent entities from ≥ 3 different eligible countries.
- [ ] **KO** ≥ 1 entity with demonstrated capacity in **youth engagement**.
- [ ] **KO** ≥ 1 entity with demonstrated capacity in **software development**.
- [ ] **KO** ≥ 1 entity with demonstrated capacity in **supporting the scaling of
      businesses or non-profits**.
- [ ] Universities ≤ 1/4 of applicants, in supportive roles, **not** the coordinator.
- [ ] No natural persons (except sole traders), no international organisations, no EU bodies.
- [ ] No entity under EU restrictive measures; no Hungarian public-interest trust under
      Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/2506 or entity it maintains.
- [ ] No exclusion situation for any applicant (Art. 138/143 FR).
- [ ] Activities take place in eligible countries.
- [ ] **No financial support to third parties** anywhere in the work plan or budget.
- [ ] **No activities directed at EU institutions** (letters, meetings, advocacy material,
      mapping of officials' positions).
- [ ] Content corresponds wholly or in part to the topic description.
- [ ] Complementarity with other EU-funded projects described.

## C. Call-specific hard requirements (section 2)

- [ ] Co-creation with youth from **≥ 8 Member States**, balanced North/East/West/South.
- [ ] Diversity addressed: gender, social background, digital/media literacy levels,
      **disabled and neurodivergent** participants explicitly included.
- [ ] Expert support from relevant disciplines and/or civil society described concretely.
- [ ] Co-creation accompanies **all stages** — ideation, development, testing, adoption —
      and the proposal shows *how* youth voice actually determines design decisions.
- [ ] **Either** ≥ 1 functioning prototype **or** upgrades to ≥ 2 existing services, on
      ActivityPub and/or AT Protocol; protocol choice justified as technically sound.
- [ ] UI look and feel co-created, not only functionality.
- [ ] Role of **AI** prominently explored — as risk and as mitigation/benefit.
- [ ] Market analysis + stakeholder mapping + uptake and sustainability strategy, with
      stakeholders involved in developing it.
- [ ] **First step of the uptake strategy implemented within the project**, with resources
      budgeted for it.
- [ ] Systematic feature comparison against other relevant social media platforms.
- [ ] ≥ 1 public report on opportunities, remaining challenges and **recommendations for
      future public funding**.
- [ ] Code open-sourced under the most appropriate licence wherever possible.
- [ ] Ethics (Art. 14 MGA) and security (Art. 13 MGA) addressed.

## D. Budget and duration (sections 3, 4, 10)

- [ ] Requested grant ≤ EUR 1 480 000.
- [ ] EU contribution ≤ **80 %** of total eligible costs; co-financing sourced and evidenced.
- [ ] Duration 12–24 months (longer only if duly justified).
- [ ] Detailed budget table and Part A summary reconciled to the cent.
- [ ] Financial capacity documents ready for every beneficiary above EUR 60 000
      (public bodies exempt).
